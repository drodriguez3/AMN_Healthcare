# IronGate | Executive demo talk track (v3)

**Opening (30 seconds):** “You already have dashboards. The question is how quickly your teams can turn data and institutional knowledge into a decision. This is a fictional illustration of that experience—not a claim about AMN’s current systems.”

**Scene 1 — Ask your documents (90 seconds):** Select “What certifications are needed for ICU nurses?” and click **Get answer from knowledge**. Point to the plain-English answer and its numbered source references. Expand a source. “Instead of making a recruiter open and interpret several documents, the copilot offers an answer they can verify. In production, Cortex Search would retrieve the relevant approved passages and an LLM would generate the grounded answer.”

**Scene 2 — AI recruiting copilot (2 minutes):** Select San Antonio and ICU RN if available; click **Generate executive briefing**. Read the priority headline, “What I found,” and the recommended actions. Open “Show the evidence behind this recommendation.” “An agent can bring together structured staffing metrics, candidate records, and policy guidance into one operational briefing. It does not make hiring decisions or contact candidates without approval.”

**Scene 3 — Ask your data (60 seconds):** Show a plain-English question answered from synthetic structured data. Avoid dwelling on SQL unless asked.

**Close (30 seconds):** “A tightly scoped pilot could validate whether this saves recruiter time, improves answer consistency, and speeds up decisions using your approved Snowflake data and knowledge sources.”

**Always disclose:** Synthetic data, fictional documents, local scripted responses. No live Cortex services or AMN connection in this version. Do not describe these results as AMN facts.
